# Hadamard exposition v2

## What the Hadamard conjecture promises

Wei Meng Soh, Whyme Labs.

This Exposition candidate explains the Hadamard conjecture through an exact coordinate-spreading objective, determinant equality and a complete prime-field Paley construction. A separately dated addendum proves a fixed-input quantization counterexample for every real Hadamard matrix of a given existing order.

The numerical study contains all 7,600 arm-level measurements from 100 seeds across 19 settings, with original, randomized Hadamard, Haar orthogonal and signed-permutation arms. At its prespecified constructed-outlier setting, mean relative squared output error changes from 2.606175% to 1.355898%. Gaussian and grid-aligned unfavorable cases are included. These are synthetic errors, not real-model quality or inference-speed measurements.

The candidate includes the main PDF, addendum PDF, standalone HTML with an exact calculator, raw records, source, tests, citation metadata and license notices. AI assistance is disclosed. Automated validation and assistant self-review are not external peer review or prize committee selection.
