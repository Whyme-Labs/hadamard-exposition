What changes when the answer stays the same

Hadamard matrices, quantization, and the price of a representation.

Wei Meng Soh · Whyme Labs

An exact calculation can stay unchanged while its rounding error changes. This article explains why, tests the idea on synthetic data and keeps the counterexamples.
