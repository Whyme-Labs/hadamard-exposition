**Author:** Wei Meng Soh, Whyme Labs. **Category:** Exposition only.

**Title:** *What changes when the answer stays the same*. **Topic:** Hadamard matrix conjecture, [MathDB problem 315867](https://mathdb.com/p/315867/hadamard-conjecture).

The submitted material is [exposition.html](exposition.html), accompanied by [article.md](article.md), [sources.json](sources.json), source code, retained numerical results and reproducible verification. `MANIFEST.json` and `SHA256SUMS` identify the exact payload. The original manuscript preparation date is 17 October 2026, not a claimed public-publication date.

## Contribution

The article explains the conjecture, the necessary divisibility condition and the Sylvester power-of-two construction. It distinguishes those known results from the unresolved universal statement. It then connects normalized Hadamard matrices to representation-dependent numerical approximation.

The contribution of this package is its explanatory treatment, worked rational counterexample, interactive calculator and paired synthetic experiment with a group-size sensitivity study. It does not claim first discovery of known identities, concentration bounds or the general idea of Hadamard-based quantization. Global novelty or priority of the illustrative counterexample has not been established.

All 60 reference records and 160 follow-up cells are retained. Gaussian and grid-aligned controls accompany the favorable outlier result. The intervals describe variation in these synthetic draws, not model-performance guarantees. The follow-up reused previously inspected seeds and is not independent confirmation on new data.
